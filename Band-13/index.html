GOES-19 Band 13: Clean Longwave Infrared (10.3 µm)

Band 13 measures thermal infrared radiation through a relatively clean atmosphere window. Meteorologists use it to monitor cloud top temperatures, cloud-
development, and storm structure during both day and night to support estimates of cloud top height. (Imagery: NOAA / NESDIS / STAR - Temperature legend: NOAA)
